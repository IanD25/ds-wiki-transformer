# Weinans et al. 2021 20-species parameter matrices

`weinans2021_full_{cA,cP,gA,gP}.csv`: the 10 x 10 competition (c) and mutualism (gamma)
matrices of the "full collapse" 20D plant-pollinator model, transcribed to 4 decimals from
the Supplementary Information (pp. 1-4) of Weinans, Quax, van Nes & van de Leemput 2021,
"Evaluating the performance of multivariate indicators of resilience loss",
Sci. Rep. 11, 9148, doi:10.1038/s41598-021-87839-y (CC BY 4.0). Transcribed 2026-10-18.

Row/column convention (not stated in the paper; read from Eqs. 1-2): gA row k = pollinator k,
column i = plant i; gP row i = plant i, column k = pollinator k. Transposing the gamma
matrices changes the start/end dominant eigenvalues by ~0.005.
