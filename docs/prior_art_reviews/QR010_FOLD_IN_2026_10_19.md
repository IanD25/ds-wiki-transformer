## 3. Links: what was linked, and what deliberately was not

- **Two `disambiguates from` links guard against the drift the QR-009 and QR-010 maps warn about.**
  - **ML1 → `gloss_participation_ratio`:** Bartlett's R_k is a participation-ratio form on the *data covariance* tail.
  - **ML2 → `gloss_effective_dimension`:** Maddox's N_eff is a soft count of *Hessian* eigenvalues.
  - Neither is the retired D_eff object (PR_FIM), and neither is Mattingly's d_eff.
- **ML2 → IT09 is not written.** S7 found the connection unsourced: it is a one-line identity, and no paper in either literature makes it.
- **No link to X6.** X6 ("Neural Networks") is about biological networks.
- **No links to QR-009's AlphaEntropy-derived items** (P18–P20). The map did not propose them.
- **ML5 → EWS4 is `couples to`, not `analogous to`.** EWS4's selection bias (conditioning on systems that tipped) and ML5's best-of-N selection are distinct mechanisms with different mathematics. The shared variable is the estimated error rate.
