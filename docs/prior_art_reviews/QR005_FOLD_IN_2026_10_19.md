## 1. S5 — reconciling the Oppenheim windows (done before drafting R3)

The map flagged that Janse et al. 2024 and Oppenheim et al. 2023 "do not reconcile". They do, once the **published** Nature Communications text is used. This session read the published text on nature.com and arXiv v1 of both papers.

| Kernel | Oppenheim et al., arXiv v1 | **Oppenheim et al., published** (Nat. Commun. 14:7910, 4 Dec 2023) | **Janse et al. 2024**, direct acceleration data on Earth only |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ultra-local continuous | 10⁻⁴¹ ≥ D₂ ≥ 10⁻²⁴ kg² s m⁻³ → excluded | 10⁻⁴¹ ≥ D₂ ≥ **10⁻⁹** → excluded | — |
| Ultra-local discrete | 10⁻¹ ≥ (l_P³/m_P)D₂ ≥ 10⁻²⁵ (24 orders) | unchanged (24 orders) | 10⁻¹⁶ ≥ … ≥ 10⁻²⁵ → **~9 orders** |
| Non-local (Diósi–Penrose kernel) | 10⁻⁹ ≥ l_P²D₂ ≥ 10⁻⁴⁰ (31 orders) | 10⁻⁹ ≥ … ≥ **10⁻³⁵** (26 orders) | 10⁻²⁴ ≥ … ≥ 10⁻³⁵ → **~11 orders** |

**What explains the mismatch:**
- **The map's "31 orders" is from arXiv v1.** The published version uses a smaller wave-packet volume (V_λ ~ 10⁻³⁷ m³) and a factor N_λ, which raises the lower bounds.
- **Janse et al. swap the labels.** Their "26 and 24 orders remaining in the ultra-local discrete model and the non-local continuous model respectively" matches the published bounds with the two models' labels swapped.
- **Janse et al.'s own windows are consistent with the published lower bounds.** Their upper bounds are 10⁻¹⁶ and 10⁻²⁴.
- **Two experiments would tighten things further, but Janse et al. are unsure they apply.** Atom interferometry would push the discrete model's upper bound below its lower bound. LISA Pathfinder would gain ~19 orders.

So the map's "~9 and ~11 orders" holds.

Other loose ends from map §I, now resolved:
- **Majorana value:** R_DP > 2.54 × 10⁻¹⁰ m at 95% CL (arXiv v5, June 2023, after the erratum).
- **XENONnT:** R₀ > 4.9 × 10⁻¹⁰ m at 90% CL and 4.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ m at 95% CL (full text).
- **Fabiano et al. 2026's threshold:** ~10⁻¹⁸ m s⁻²/√Hz at 1 mm, "about a factor of 1000 below the LISA Pathfinder level" (text).
